Rent with care.

  • Admin approval is a content review. It does not verify identity, ownership, availability, item safety, or a successful transaction.
  • Confirm the owner's identity and inspect the item. Discuss known defects and safe use before agreeing to rent.
  • Agree in writing on rental dates, total price, deposit refund, delivery fees, late fees, damage responsibility, and return arrangements. Keep your own receipts.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, or ID documents in a listing. Choose a safe handover arrangement and be cautious about advance-payment requests.
  • Offer only items you own or are authorized to rent out. Do not list stolen, illegal, dangerous, or misleading items.
  • Use Report listing for spam, incorrect details, privacy concerns, or removal requests. Reports go to the admin; this is not an emergency service.

HiramMuna PH helps people discover listings and contact each other. It does not process payments, hold deposits, provide insurance, or settle rental agreements.

Need to change your listing?

Use the reference and private change-request code in your saved submission confirmation. The admin reviews requests before changing or removing an item.

For an older public listing without a private code, open its details and use Report listing. Never share the admin key.

Your listing and privacy.

We store your submitted item details, optional resized photo, Messenger link, phone number, publication permission, review status, and submission time. Pending and rejected items are accessible to the admin. Approved items and consented contact details are public and may be copied by others. Use a business contact if you prefer.

Reports store the listing reference, reason, details, status, and time; reports are private to the admin. Please do not put personal IDs, full addresses, or sensitive information in descriptions, photos, or reports.

Cloudflare hosts this website and database. Its Turnstile anti-spam check processes browser/network signals. A salted hourly identifier based on your connection is stored briefly for rate limiting; raw IP addresses are not stored in our listings database. Hosting providers may separately keep technical logs. Cloudflare privacy policy ↗

Owners currently request changes through the admin, rather than a self-service account. For a public listing, use Report listing → Privacy or removal request and include its reference. Admins can edit, unpublish, or delete listings. Saved submission confirmations also include a private change-request code for a pending or rejected listing.

Listings stay until the admin deletes them. Reports stay until removed by the admin. Rate-limit identifiers expire after their hourly window and are cleaned up on later submissions. There is no advertising tracker or owner login in this version.
